Fermentation Extends Life!

Hands-on dry fermentation with Li Bo, Chi Garden Ecological Farm

Join us in the catering kitchen at the Just Food Community Farm for a hands-on workshop on dry fermentation — an approach used in the eastern Himalayan region — using organic semi-dried vegetables, herbs and spices. Each participant packs their own jar and takes it home.
