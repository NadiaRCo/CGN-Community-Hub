# CGN Community Hub — what I built, why, and what you still need to do

*Test build for Kate's review. Nothing here has been published or sent.*

## 1. What other garden networks do well (research)
- **Just Food's own CGN pages** (the model to match and avoid duplicating): About the CGN, Start a Community Garden, Find a Garden (map + profiles), Gardening Resources (Garden Guide in 5 languages, pre-recorded workshops, accessible gardening, soil testing), Upcoming Workshops. Their weakness: slides and past workshops are not kept anywhere, there is no calendar, and there is no way to see who uses what. Your Hub can fill exactly those gaps.
- **Capital Growth (London)**: a free membership network with a clear "what you get" list, toolkits and guides, training, and site visits. Lesson: say plainly what members receive, and keep toolkits in one place.
- **Denver Urban Gardens / Seattle P-Patch / Cornell / ACGA**: strong "garden leader" support — leadership training, handbooks, succession. Lesson: coordinators are a separate audience from gardeners and need their own page.
- **Sustain Ontario Community Growing Network, TCHC**: shared templates (e.g. community agreements) and a list of who to call. Lesson: templates for coordinators are the most-wanted resource.
- **Garden-leader trainings (NC State, Illinois Extension)**: season planning, volunteers, rules, communication, funding.

## 2. What I built and why
| Page | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| Home: quick-start tiles, article of the week, calendar, upcoming workshops | Visitors arrive with different goals (find a plot, start a garden, learn, coordinate); tiles route them in one click |
| About the CGN | Plain-language explanation and the "benefits of joining" list adapted from the Just Food site |
| Find a Garden | Points to the existing map, explains pins, waitlists and garden types |
| Start a Garden | The 7-step path (workshop → team → support → land → CGDF → soil) |
| For Coordinators | Separate audience; lists what the CGN offers plus a skeleton of coordinator tools "coming soon" |
| Gardening Guides | Garden Guide in 5 languages, free recorded workshops, Ottawa season-at-a-glance table |
| Workshops: upcoming / past / slides | Your original request; slides are the engagement hook |
| CGN Resources | 29 verified links, searchable, tagged |
| FAQ | Answers the questions the inbox probably gets repeatedly |
| Contact the CGN | Replaces "suggest a topic": opens email to communitygardening@justfood.ca; also lists phone and address |
| Calendar | Workshops appear automatically; add other dates in the `events` list |

Other features: EN/FR toggle (funding requirement), colour and font pickers from Nadia's Hub, engagement tracking (see Setup guide), keyboard-accessible menu.
