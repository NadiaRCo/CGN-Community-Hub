## What it is

The CGN Hub is one friendly home for gardeners: workshops and slides, the Garden Guide in five languages, resources, FAQs, a garden almanac with the local forecast, and a weekly article. It is bilingual (EN/FR) and works on a phone.

## How to review it (about 15 minutes)

Please open it as a gardener would and click around. Some suggested stops:

1. **Home:** the welcome garden, the almanac (frost, rain and this month's garden jobs) and this week's article.
2. **Article of the Week:** "Composting!" is this week's article. Three earlier articles sit below it (companion planting, pest control and bucket gardening). All four come from the CGN Garden Guide.
3. **About the CGN:** new dropdowns from the Garden Guide: What is the CGN?, the CGDF, A community garden is…, and the benefits of community gardens.
4. **Past workshops and Slides:** the summer series, with slides and handouts opening in Google Drive.
5. **Gardening Guides, Resources, FAQs:** links to Just Food pages.
6. **FR button** (top right) to see the French version.
